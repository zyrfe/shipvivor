The industrial yards

Bridge-camera studies from Longnight Road and Boreal Road, on run seed 40. Original concept sheet

Round 5 · The Red Shift

Two ivory governors control the charging gallery. When both fail, the hopper tips and the sawtooth roof folds bay by bay. The fuel tank beside the mill can take a quay gun with it.

Round 6 · The Bell Works

The hammer strikes once every 3.4 seconds. Smash both governors and it drops through the forge bed. Behind the dock, the cliff shield lifts and the twin gun carriage rolls out.

Approach check

On Longnight Road and Boreal Road, a cruiser held station for 30 seconds on each approach. At 390 metres from the yard, only the outer picket fired. At 250 metres on the same bearing, all five light mounts opened up. Both trials used run seed 42.
