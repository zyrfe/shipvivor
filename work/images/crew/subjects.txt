gunnery|THE GUNNERY OFFICER. A poised, precise woman around 30 with pale skin and steel-grey eyes, platinum-blonde hair in a long single braid over one shoulder, a brass stereoscopic rangefinder eyepiece pushed up on her brow like goggles. Tailored navy tailcoat with gold aiguillettes looped across the chest and crimson-lined lapels, spotless white gloves, one hand holding a brass range-table slide rule. Cool, exacting half-smile, the look of someone who has already worked out where your ship will be.
signals|THE SIGNALS OFFICER, called Sparks. An eager young man in his mid-20s with tousled copper-red hair, a spray of freckles and round wire spectacles, bakelite radio headphones pushed down around his neck, a pencil tucked behind one ear. Navy imperial jacket worn unbuttoned over a crimson waistcoat, gold lightning-bolt signal badges on the high collar, a slip of radio message paper in his fingers. Bright, alert, mid-thought, as if he just caught a faint contact on the set.
engineer|THE CHIEF ENGINEER. A broad-shouldered, formidable South Asian woman in her late 40s, dark hair streaked with grey tied up under a dark kerchief, a smudge of soot on one cheek, laugh lines. A grand gold-braided navy waistcoat with a brass pocket-watch chain over a shirt with sleeves rolled to the elbow, strong forearms, a heavy brass-and-steel spanner resting on one shoulder and a small brass pressure gauge pinned like a medal. Warm, wry, unflappable grin of someone who has kept the boilers lit through worse.
first-lieutenant|THE FIRST LIEUTENANT, who leads damage control. A tall, steady Black man in his mid-30s with close-cropped hair and a neat short beard, a high-collared navy greatcoat with gold epaulettes and a crimson damage-control armband, a gleaming ornate brass fire-brigade helmet with a crest held under one arm, a coil of canvas hose over the other shoulder. Calm, dependable, reassuring gaze; the man who holds the ship together when she is burning.
liaison|THE LIAISON OFFICER, who calls in bombers and fleet gunfire. A confident, dashing East Asian woman around 30 with a short sleek black bob, a leather flying helmet pushed back with aviator goggles on top, a long white silk scarf, a brown leather flight jacket thrown over the shoulders of a gold-braided navy dress tunic with silver aviator's wings on the breast, a brass field-telephone handset in one gloved hand. A sly, knowing smirk; she has friends over the horizon.
